# Candlestick Pattern Catalog

Detailed reference for 40+ patterns. Each entry gives the **structure** (candle-by-candle), **what it signals**, a **strength** rating, and — where useful — a **trade** note. Always respect trend context: reversal patterns only "count" after a move in the opposite direction; continuation patterns need an established trend.

For entries and stops, the general rule (from SKILL.md) applies: enter on the confirming candle's close, place the stop just beyond the pattern's extreme (below the low for bullish, above the high for bearish), and target the next support/resistance. Trade notes below only add pattern-specific detail.

## Bullish reversal patterns

Appear during a **downtrend** and hint at a turn upward.

### Bullish Engulfing (2 candles) — Strength: High
**Structure:** A small bearish candle, then a larger bullish candle whose body completely engulfs the prior body (closes above the prior open).
**Signals:** Buyers decisively overwhelmed sellers — a strong bullish reversal.
**Trade:** Most reliable at a support level after a sustained downtrend, with the bullish candle's volume exceeding the prior candle's. Enter on its close or the next open.

### Bullish Marubozu (1 candle) — Strength: Medium-High
**Structure:** A long bullish candle with essentially no wicks — opens at the low, closes at the high.
**Signals:** Total buyer control for the whole session; strong momentum, often a breakout.
**Trade:** Strongest with a volume surge and at/through a key level. Confirmation is a close beyond the candle's high. Fast, aggressive candles — set risk upfront and avoid chasing an already-extended one.

### Hammer (1 candle) — Strength: Medium (needs confirmation)
**Structure:** A small body near the top with a lower shadow at least ~2× the body, little or no upper shadow.
**Signals:** Sellers pushed price down intraday but buyers drove it back to close near the open — rejected selling. In a downtrend, buyers are gaining control.
**Trade:** Wait for the next candle to close higher; stop below the hammer's low. A green-bodied hammer is slightly more bullish than a red one, but position and wick length matter more than color.

### Morning Star (3 candles) — Strength: High
**Structure:** A long bearish candle; a small-bodied "star" (bullish or bearish) that gaps down; a long bullish candle closing well into the first candle's body.
**Signals:** Downtrend → indecision → buyers take over. A strong reversal.
**Trade:** Valid when the third candle closes at least halfway into the first's body. Strongest when the star is a doji (a **Morning Doji Star**); volume typically expands on the third candle.

### Piercing Line (2 candles) — Strength: Medium
**Structure:** A strong bearish candle, then a bullish candle that opens *below* the prior close but closes *above the midpoint (50%)* of the prior bearish body.
**Signals:** Buyers stepping in forcefully — bullish reversal. (Bullish mirror of Dark Cloud Cover.)

### Bullish Harami (2 candles) — Strength: Low-Medium (early warning)
**Structure:** A large bearish candle, then a small bullish candle whose body sits entirely within the prior body. ("Harami" = pregnant in Japanese.) If the second candle is a doji, it's a **Bullish Harami Cross** (a stronger variant).
**Signals:** Selling has slowed and buyers are stirring — a possible *slowdown* more than an immediate reversal.
**Trade:** Weaker than an engulfing; use as an alert to watch for bullish confirmation next, not a direct entry.

### Three White Soldiers (3 candles) — Strength: High
**Structure:** Three consecutive long bullish candles with small/absent upper wicks; each opens within the prior body and closes progressively higher.
**Signals:** Sustained, orderly buying — a decisive shift up.
**Trade:** Best after selling or consolidation. Large upper wicks weaken it; very large candles after an extended move can signal exhaustion instead.

### Inverted Hammer (1 candle) — Strength: Medium
**Structure:** Small body with a long *upper* shadow and little/no lower shadow, at the bottom of a downtrend.
**Signals:** Buyers attempted to push price up during the session — early reversal sign; needs next-candle confirmation.

### Dragonfly Doji (1 candle) — Strength: Medium
**Structure:** Open, high, and close all near the same (upper) level, with a long lower shadow and essentially **no body**.
**Signals:** Sellers were rejected and price returned to the open — potential bullish reversal at a bottom.

### Bullish Abandoned Baby (3 candles) — Strength: High (rare)
**Structure:** A long bearish candle; a doji that **gaps down** (isolated, gaps on both sides); a long bullish candle that **gaps up**.
**Signals:** A sharp, strong shift from bearish to bullish sentiment. Rare but reliable.

### Three Inside Up (3 candles) — Strength: Medium-High
**Structure:** A large bearish candle; a small bullish candle closing above the 50% level of the first (a Harami); a bullish candle closing above the first candle's open.
**Signals:** Confirmed bullish reversal.

### Three Outside Up (3 candles) — Strength: High
**Structure:** A bearish candle; a bullish candle that engulfs it (Bullish Engulfing); a third bullish candle closing higher.
**Signals:** Confirms the strength of a bullish reversal.

### Bullish Kicker (2 candles) — Strength: High
**Structure:** A long bearish candle, then a long bullish candle that **gaps up** — opens above the prior open and rises further, with no overlap.
**Signals:** An abrupt, powerful sentiment shift; one of the strongest reversal signals.

### Tweezer Bottom (2 candles) — Strength: Medium-High
**Structure:** Two candles (typically bearish then bullish) sharing the same/near-identical **lows**.
**Signals:** Buyers defended the same price twice — support found.
**Trade:** Strongest when the matched low aligns with a prior support zone and the bullish second candle has higher volume.

### Concealing Baby Swallow (4 candles) — Strength: Medium (rare)
**Structure:** Two long bearish candles; a candle gapping down with a small body; a final long bearish candle that fully engulfs the small one.
**Signals:** Selling pressure exhausting itself — potential bullish reversal ahead. Rare.

### Bullish Belt Hold (1 candle) — Strength: Medium
**Structure:** At a downtrend bottom, a long bullish candle opening at/near the low (no lower shadow), closing near the high.
**Signals:** Strong buying from the open; potential reversal.

### Ladder Bottom (5 candles) — Strength: Medium
**Structure:** Three consecutive long bearish candles, then a small bearish/bullish candle, then a long bullish candle.
**Signals:** The bearish trend is ending and buyers may be taking control.

### Meeting Lines — Bullish (2 candles) — Strength: Medium
**Structure:** A long bearish candle, then a long bullish candle that opens lower but closes at the **same level** as the prior candle's close.
**Signals:** Shift from selling to buying pressure — bullish reversal after a downtrend.

## Bullish continuation patterns

Appear during an **uptrend** and signal it will likely resume after a pause.

### Rising Three Methods (5 candles) — Strength: Medium-High
**Structure:** A long bullish candle; three small bearish candles that stay *within* the range of the first; then a long bullish candle closing above the first's high.
**Signals:** A brief pullback, then the uptrend continues — buyers still in control.

### Mat Hold (5 candles) — Strength: Medium-High
**Structure:** Like Rising Three Methods but with a shallower pullback (often gapping): a long bullish candle, three small bearish candles within its range, then a long bullish candle closing above the first's high.
**Signals:** Brief consolidation within an uptrend before continuation.

### Bullish Separating Lines (2 candles) — Strength: Medium
**Structure:** A bearish candle, then a bullish candle that opens at the **same level** as the prior candle's open.
**Signals:** After a brief counter-move, the uptrend continues.

### Bullish Three-Line Strike (4 candles) — Strength: Medium
**Structure:** Three consecutive bullish candles, then a long bearish candle that opens higher and closes below the first candle of the pattern (erasing the three gains in one candle).
**Signals:** Despite the sharp bearish candle, price is expected to resume upward after the pause.

## Bearish reversal patterns

Appear during an **uptrend** and hint at a turn downward.

### Bearish Engulfing (2 candles) — Strength: High
**Structure:** A small bullish candle, then a larger bearish candle whose body completely engulfs the prior body (closes below the prior open).
**Signals:** Sellers took decisive control — price may continue to fall.
**Trade:** Most reliable at a resistance level after a sustained uptrend; higher volume on the bearish candle strengthens it. Stop above the pattern's high.

### Bearish Marubozu (1 candle) — Strength: Medium-High
**Structure:** A long bearish candle with essentially no wicks — opens at the high, closes at the low.
**Signals:** Total seller control for the session; strong downside momentum, often a breakdown.
**Trade:** Strongest with a volume surge and at/through a key level; confirmation is a close below the candle's low.

### Bearish Belt Hold (1 candle) — Strength: Medium
**Structure:** During an uptrend, a long bearish candle opening at/near the high (no upper shadow), closing near the low.
**Signals:** Strong selling from the open; potential reversal down.

### Hanging Man (1 candle) — Strength: Medium (needs confirmation)
**Structure:** At an uptrend top, a small body with a long lower shadow and little/no upper shadow — same shape as a Hammer, but location makes it bearish.
**Signals:** Sellers were able to push price down mid-session despite the uptrend; momentum may be turning.
**Trade:** Needs the next candle to close lower; stop above the hanging man's high. Stronger at resistance or after a prolonged advance.

### Shooting Star (1 candle) — Strength: Medium (needs confirmation)
**Structure:** At an uptrend top, a small body with a long upper shadow (≥2× body) and little/no lower shadow. Mirror image of the Hammer.
**Signals:** Buyers pushed price up but sellers rejected it and closed near the open.
**Trade:** Look for it at resistance or after a strong up-move; a bearish confirmation candle strengthens it. Stop above the star's high.

### Gravestone Doji (1 candle) — Strength: Medium
**Structure:** Open, low, and close all near the same (lower) level, with a long upper shadow and essentially **no body**. The bearish counterpart to the Dragonfly Doji.
**Signals:** Buyers were fully rejected at the highs — potential reversal down at a top.

### Bearish Harami (2 candles) — Strength: Low-Medium (early warning)
**Structure:** A large bullish candle, then a small bearish candle whose body sits entirely within the prior body. If the second candle is a doji, it's a **Bearish Harami Cross** (stronger variant).
**Signals:** Buying pressure waning — sellers not yet decisive.
**Trade:** Use as an alert for bearish confirmation next, not a direct entry.

### Bearish Doji Star (2 candles) — Strength: Medium
**Structure:** A long bullish candle, then a doji (very small body), typically gapping up.
**Signals:** Indecision after a rally; a bearish candle next confirms a potential downtrend.

### Evening Star (3 candles) — Strength: High
**Structure:** A long bullish candle; a small-bodied star gapping up; a long bearish candle closing well into the first's body. Bearish mirror of the Morning Star.
**Signals:** Uptrend peaks, momentum stalls, sellers take control.
**Trade:** Most reliable when the third candle closes more than halfway into the first; the **Evening Doji Star** (doji middle) is the stronger variant.

### Bearish Abandoned Baby (3 candles) — Strength: High (rare)
**Structure:** A long bullish candle; a doji that **gaps up** (isolated); a long bearish candle that **gaps down**.
**Signals:** A sharp reversal and the start of a downtrend. Rare but strong.

### Upside Gap Two Crows (3 candles) — Strength: Medium
**Structure:** A long bullish candle; two small bearish candles that gap up; the second bearish candle closes below the first's close.
**Signals:** Potential reversal or brief consolidation before a downtrend continues.

### Bearish Tweezer Top (2+ candles) — Strength: Medium-High
**Structure:** Two or more candles with matching **highs** at an uptrend top — typically a bullish candle then a bearish candle.
**Signals:** Upward momentum stalling at resistance; reversal may follow.

### Bearish Kicker (2 candles) — Strength: High
**Structure:** A long bullish candle, then a long bearish candle that **gaps down** — opens below the prior open, closes lower.
**Signals:** A dramatic, sudden shift to the downside; one of the strongest bearish signals.

### Three Inside Down (3 candles) — Strength: Medium-High
**Structure:** A bullish candle; a smaller bearish candle contained within it (a Harami); a bearish candle closing lower.
**Signals:** Sellers gaining dominance — confirms a bearish reversal.

### Three Outside Down (3 candles) — Strength: High
**Structure:** A bullish candle; a bearish candle that engulfs it (Bearish Engulfing); a third bearish candle closing lower.
**Signals:** Confirms the strength of a bearish reversal.

### Dark Cloud Cover (2 candles) — Strength: Medium
**Structure:** A long bullish candle, then a bearish candle that opens *above* the prior high but closes *below the midpoint* of the prior bullish body. Bearish counterpart to the Piercing Line.
**Signals:** The uptrend may be over and a downtrend beginning.

### Three Black Crows (3 candles) — Strength: High
**Structure:** Three consecutive long bearish candles with small/absent lower wicks, each closing progressively lower, each opening within the prior body.
**Signals:** Sustained selling — a decisive turn down at an uptrend top (and continued selling thereafter).
**Trade:** Strongest near resistance after an uptrend; small lower wicks are ideal. Very large candles after an extended fall can mean oversold conditions rather than more downside.

## Bearish continuation patterns

Appear during a **downtrend** and signal it will likely resume.

### Falling Three Methods (5 candles) — Strength: Medium-High
**Structure:** A long bearish candle; three small bullish candles that stay *within* the range of the first (a weak upward drift); then a long bearish candle closing below the first's low. Bearish mirror of Rising Three Methods.
**Signals:** A brief pause/bounce, then the downtrend resumes — sellers still in control.

### Bearish Three-Line Strike (4 candles) — Strength: Medium
**Structure:** Three consecutive bearish candles, then a long bullish candle that opens lower and closes above the first candle's open (erasing the three losses).
**Signals:** Despite the bullish fourth candle, a short pullback before the downtrend continues.

### Bearish Mat Hold (5 candles) — Strength: Medium-High
**Structure:** A long bearish candle; three small bullish candles staying within the first's range; another long bearish candle closing below the first.
**Signals:** A brief pause before the downtrend continues.

## Neutral / indecision patterns

Signal a balance between buyers and sellers. Meaning depends on context and what follows — treat as alerts, not standalone triggers.

### Doji (1 candle) — Strength: Low alone; High with context
**Structure:** Open and close at/near the same price — a thin body forming a cross or plus sign.
**Signals:** Neither side won the session — indecision. A doji after a prolonged trend, at a key level, can mark exhaustion and a turning point.
**Variants:** **Dragonfly** (long lower wick, no upper — bullish at bottoms), **Gravestone** (long upper wick, no lower — bearish at tops), **Long-legged** (long wicks both sides — extreme indecision).
**Trade:** Context is everything; combine with volume, moving averages, or the surrounding trend.

### Long-legged Doji (1 candle) — Strength: Low-Medium
**Structure:** A doji with long upper *and* lower shadows.
**Signals:** Extreme indecision — large swings both ways that resolved back at the open. Often precedes a volatility shift or reversal at extremes.

### Spinning Top (1 candle) — Strength: Low alone; Medium in clusters
**Structure:** A small body centered between roughly equal upper and lower wicks (body color less important than its small size).
**Signals:** Both sides fought but neither prevailed — the session ended near where it opened. A single one isn't tradeable; a cluster after a directional move suggests the trend is losing momentum.

### Inside Bar (2 candles) — Strength: Medium (breakout direction decides)
**Structure:** The second candle's entire range (high to low, wicks included) is contained within the first candle's range — the first is the "mother bar."
**Signals:** Consolidation/coiling within the prior range; neither side has broken out. The eventual break beyond the mother bar's high or low signals the next directional move.
**Trade:** Traders often set a buy stop above the mother bar's high and a sell stop below its low, taking whichever breaks first. More reliable when the mother bar is a strong directional candle and on daily+ charts.

## Easily-confused pairs

- **Hammer vs Hanging Man** — identical shape (small body, long lower shadow). *Location decides:* Hammer at a downtrend bottom = bullish; Hanging Man at an uptrend top = bearish.
- **Hammer vs Dragonfly Doji** — both have long lower shadows at a bottom. The Hammer has a small *real body*; the Dragonfly Doji has essentially *no body* (open ≈ close).
- **Inverted Hammer vs Shooting Star** — identical shape (small body, long upper shadow). Inverted Hammer at a bottom = bullish; Shooting Star at a top = bearish.
- **Shooting Star vs Gravestone Doji** — both have a long upper wick at a top. The Shooting Star keeps a small body; the Gravestone Doji has no body (open ≈ close ≈ low).
- **Doji vs Spinning Top** — both signal indecision. A Doji has essentially no body (open ≈ close); a Spinning Top has a small but real body between two wicks.
- **Morning Star vs Evening Star** — mirror images. Morning Star (bearish→small→bullish) is a bottom reversal; Evening Star (bullish→small→bearish) is a top reversal. Doji-middle variants are stronger.
- **Piercing Line vs Dark Cloud Cover** — mirror images across the prior candle's midpoint. Piercing Line is bullish (closes above the midpoint of a prior *red* candle); Dark Cloud Cover is bearish (closes below the midpoint of a prior *green* candle).
- **Engulfing vs Harami** — opposite geometries. Engulfing: the second candle is *larger* and swallows the first (decisive). Harami: the second is *smaller* and sits inside the first (hesitation, early warning).
- **Engulfing vs Kicker** — Engulfing overlaps and swallows the prior body; the Kicker *gaps* away entirely (no overlap), making it a stronger, more abrupt signal.
- **Marubozu vs long candle** — a Marubozu has essentially *no wicks* (open and close at the extremes); a merely long candle still has shadows. Wicks reduce a Marubozu's "total control" reading.
- **Rising Three Methods vs Mat Hold** — both bullish continuation (long candle, three small counter-candles, long candle). Mat Hold's pullback is shallower and often gaps; treat them as close cousins. Their bearish mirrors are Falling Three Methods and Bearish Mat Hold.
